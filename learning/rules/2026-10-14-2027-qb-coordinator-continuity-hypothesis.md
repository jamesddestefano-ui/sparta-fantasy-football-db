# Sparta-only 2027 QB draft hypothesis — proposed, not adopted

Date: 2026-10-14 ET
Scope: Billy's 2026 Sparta fantasy football league only, future 2027 auction preparation.
Status: PROPOSED / TEST REQUIRED. No historical decision or rule rewritten.

## User observation
Quarterback auction spending should be minimized unless paying for a genuinely differentiated top-tier fantasy QB. Evaluate offensive-coordinator/play-caller continuity and scheme fit before drafting QB, rather than relying primarily on name recognition or previous-year fantasy totals. User cited Jalen Hurts adapting to 2026 Eagles offensive coordinator Sean Mannion; specific shotgun restrictions remain unverified.

## Existing approach / proposed change
Old approach: No verified general prohibition on paying for quarterbacks; draft prices and projection tiers considered.
Proposed approach: In single-QB Sparta auction, lean late/cheap QB unless a high-confidence, materially superior projected starter offers adequate surplus value. Add explicit offensive-coordinator stability/scheme fit checklist: coordinator tenure, first-time play-caller status, system changes, shotgun rate, designed QB runs, passing pace, red-zone QB opportunities, offensive-line and receiver continuity, projected replacement QB production.

## Evidence and uncertainty
2026 Sparta auction record: Jalen Hurts drafted for $7 (user recollection $8; authoritative draft board $7). Philadelphia Eagles officially hired Sean Mannion January 2026 as first-time play-caller and reported new offensive scheme. Sources: https://www.philadelphiaeagles.com/news/eagles-2026-offense-sean-mannion-jalen-hurts-dave-spadaro and https://www.philadelphiaeagles.com/news/jalen-hurts-eagles-do-what-it-takes-to-win . No demonstrated causal estimate that new OC reduces fantasy output; no verified claim Mannion prohibited Hurts from shotgun.

Supporting decisions: None yet graded as conclusive. Sample size: one user-proposed 2026 case study (Hurts), insufficient for a hard rule.
Expected effect: Reduce QB auction overspend and avoid unpriced scheme-transition risk while reserving FAAB/draft budget for RB/WR value.

## Prospective validation
Backtest at least several seasons of single-QB fantasy production against OC retention/new hires while controlling for injuries, rushing profile, roster changes, QB age and ADP; compare late-QB vs premium-QB auction surplus value in Sparta-style scoring. Track weeks 1-5 versus rest of season and avoid retroactive fit. Only promote hypothesis to adopted rule with repeated supporting results.
